The problem

The job is spread across tools. The numbers should not be.

One shared plan.

Budget, WBS, plan, hours and purchases live in the project, not in separate spreadsheets and e-mails.

Hours valued on the day they were worked.

Approved hours become actual cost of the WBS, valued at the hourly cost valid on the day.

Committed cost before the invoice.

Open purchase orders show the committed cost, so the loss of margin is visible before the job closes.

AI in daily work

Ask your project data

Copilot and Cowork answer questions in natural language, read-only, with the same security as the app. The Portfolio Agent brings ProjectIQ cards into Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Copilot Chat“How is project PRJ-000123 going?”An answer built from the same records you see in the app.
Project manager“Which hours do I have to approve?”Read-only: every action stays on the buttons of the app.
Portfolio Agent“Create a draft project from this document”A draft to complete; nothing is created before Confirm.

AI can make mistakes. Check the answers; Copilot reads only the data your role can already see.

Frequently asked questions

Which licences do I need?

Power Apps Premium for internal users. Suppliers use the Power Pages portal.

Where are my data stored?

In your company's own Dataverse environment: one database per company, nothing shared with other customers.

Can team members see costs and margins?

Only users with the OP365 Cost Visibility column security profile see hourly costs and the other protected cost columns.

Can Copilot change data?

No. Copilot and Cowork are read-only and see only the data your role can already access. The Portfolio Agent logs hours or creates a draft project only after you confirm.

Which languages are available?

The app and the manuals are in English, Italian, French and German.

Is it open source?

Yes, under the Apache License 2.0. The source is on GitHub.
